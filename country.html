Method. Daily closes on days both the country index and MSCI World have a close. Each local index is divided by that day's USD exchange rate, so currency moves count, as they do for a DR holder. RS = index in USD ÷ MSCI World. RS-Ratio = 100 + z-score of RS over 60 sessions; RS-Momentum = 100 + z-score of the 10-session change in RS-Ratio. Leading = both at or above 100; Weakening = RS-Ratio at or above 100, RS-Momentum below; Lagging = both below 100; Improving = RS-Ratio below 100, RS-Momentum at or above. Rows are ordered Leading, Weakening, Improving, Lagging, strongest RS-Ratio first. Europe is one row for the DRs on French, German, Italian, Dutch and Danish stocks, measured by the STOXX Europe 600. Sources: MSCI (MSCI World), Yahoo Finance (indices, FX), VNDirect (VN-Index).
