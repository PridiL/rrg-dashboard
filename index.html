Panels. United States (147), Hong Kong (55), Japan (26), China (9), Vietnam (8), Singapore (9), Europe (8). Taiwan is omitted: all three Taiwanese DRs track ETFs, so nothing survives the stocks-only filter.

Method. RS = 100 × (price ÷ benchmark) on shared bar timestamps. RS-Ratio = 100 + the z-score of RS over a 60-day window; RS-Momentum = 100 + the z-score of the 10-day rate of change of RS-Ratio, same window. JdK's published RS-Ratio and RS-Momentum formulas are proprietary, so this is a standard z-score replication — quadrant placement and rotation direction track the original closely, absolute values will not match exactly. Treat anything within ~0.3 of a boundary as unclassified.

Currency. Computed on the underlying listings in their own currency, not on the THB DR prices. Each DR's THB price is underlying × FX ÷ conversion ratio, and within a country panel both FX and ratio are common constants, so they cancel out of relative strength exactly — the local-currency result is the same rotation against a local benchmark, with less noise. Rotation therefore does not reflect your FX exposure.

Deduplication. Multiple DRs on one company collapse to a single mark under the underlying ticker — AAPL01/03/19/80 plot once as AAPL. Hover a mark to see which DRs it covers.

Marks share one colour by design: quadrant is carried by position and by the labelled regions, because the Leading↔Lagging colour pair fails colourblind separation (ΔE 4.1 under deuteranopia).
